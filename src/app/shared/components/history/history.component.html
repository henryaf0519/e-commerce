Our Roots

Born in the Andes.
Crafted for the epicurean.

Root & Cane was born in the cobblestone streets of Zapatoca, Colombia — a mountain town where sugar cane has been harvested and transformed into confections for generations.

Don Pablo began perfecting these recipes in 1965. Each sweet carries the memory of open-air kitchens, copper pots, and the unhurried patience of hands that know what they're doing.

We bring these traditions to the Bay Area's most discerning palates — entirely free of high-fructose corn syrup, GMOs, and artificial additives.
